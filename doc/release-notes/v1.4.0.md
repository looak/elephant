## v1.4.0

The pieces cycle, the fourth evaluation cycle since 1.0. PeSTO values each piece on its own; 1.4 adds what
pieces are worth together, refits every table at once, and for the first time fits them on Elephant's own games.

Most of the gain came from fitting, not from new terms. Once the tables were fitted together, terms that overlap them
(rooks on open files, outposts) had nothing left to add and were left out.

### Evaluation
- **Piece pairs:** the bishop pair (bishops on both colours), and two or more knights or rooks. The bishop pair is the
  largest, about half a pawn in the endgame.
- **Rebalance:** every table fitted together but material, the king's table and the king danger table. Two safeguards
  in `ElephantTuner` keep a joint fit from re-pricing the pieces: each tuned piece square table stays centred on the
  average it started with, so only its shape moves (`--free-psqt` turns it off), and values few positions use are
  kept as they are (`--min-usage N`).
- **Fine-tuned on Elephant's own games:** 1.2M positions from the games Elephant played in testing, stored in nyayo,
  on top of the rebalance. It fits Elephant's games better and the E12.52 dataset worse, and won in play.
- **Mop-up** rewritten in the style of the other terms, in its own file with an exact integer gate. Same search, node
  for node.

### Under the hood
- Zero compiler warnings, stronger warning flags, warnings as errors in CI (PR #32).

### Tried, not merged
- Mop-up driving the losing king to the edge, everywhere or only without pawns: the pawnless version passed at short
  time control and failed at long on endgame openings; the score jumped when the last pawn came off.
- Rooks and queens on open & half-open files: −13.8 on the old tables, flat on the rebalanced ones.
- Knight & bishop outposts: −3.0 ± 6.0 after 6512 games, stopped.

### OpenBench
Each change against the one before it, 1 thread. Short time control first, then a long one to confirm. The rebalance
and the fine-tune were tested on UHO_Lichess_4852_v1, whose unbalanced openings stretch Elo next to 8moves_v3.
